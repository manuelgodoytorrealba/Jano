# VISION.md — JANO

## What JANO Is

JANO is a premium digital platform for discovering, understanding, and connecting art through visual exploration, relationships, and curated knowledge.

It is not just a database of artworks or artists.  
It is a living system of meaning.

JANO helps people move from isolated information to connected understanding.

---

# Core Mission

Make art easier to explore, deeper to understand, and more meaningful to return to.

JANO should help users go from:

- “I know this artwork”
  to
- “I understand why it matters”

and from:

- “I know this artist”
  to
- “I can connect movements, ideas, symbols, influences, and context”

---

# Long-Term Vision (3+ Years)

Build one of the best digital environments in the world for art discovery and connected learning.

A place where users can:

- discover new artists visually
- explore relationships between works, concepts, movements, places, and time periods
- study art in a modern way
- save personal collections
- receive intelligent recommendations
- follow curiosity endlessly
- learn through interaction instead of static reading
- trust high-quality editorial curation

JANO should feel closer to a premium product experience than a traditional museum archive.

# What Makes JANO Unique

## 1. Connected Knowledge

Entities are not isolated pages.  
Everything can connect to everything else.

Artists, works, movements, concepts, materials, places, periods, articles, and ideas form a navigable knowledge network.

## 2. Visual First

Discovery should begin with images, composition, atmosphere, and curiosity.

Not with walls of text.

## 3. Premium Experience

Elegant interface, refined interactions, calm confidence, strong hierarchy, intentional motion.

## 4. Human Curation + Smart Systems

Quality comes from editorial decisions supported by intelligent tooling.

## 5. Built for Return Visits

Users should want to come back repeatedly, not just visit once.

# Decision Filter

When building something, ask:

- Does this improve discovery?
- Does this improve understanding?
- Does this improve beauty or clarity?
- Does this create long-term leverage?
- Would users return because of this?
- Is this premium quality?
- Is it coherent with JANO?

If not, reconsider.
